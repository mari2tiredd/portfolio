July 28, 1917 Civic Action

The Silent Protest Parade

Nearly 10,000 African Americans march silently down Fifth Avenue to the beat of muffled drums, protesting racial violence and lynchings. Organized by W.E.B. Du Bois and the NAACP, this disciplined demonstration marks the political awakening of the New Negro Movement.

Cultural Ripple Effect

Established Harlem as the intellectual and political nerve center of Black America, setting the stage for artistic self-determination.
